# India Labelling Rules (mock)
> FICTIONAL MOCK RULES for a student project. Not real law.

## IND-LAB-1: Required label fields
Every packaged consumer product sold in India must show the product name, net quantity, a batch number, the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) inclusive of all taxes, and the name and address of the manufacturer or importer. Cosmetics and supplements must also list ingredients.

## IND-LAB-2: Prohibited medical claims
Labels must not claim that a product cures, treats or prevents a disease. Words such as "cure", "treats", "miracle" or "stops hair loss" are not allowed.

## IND-LAB-3: Substantiated claims
Any claim such as "clinically proven" must be backed by a test report kept on file. Without a report, the claim must be removed from the label.

## IND-LAB-4: Language Requirements
Key information must be printed in English or Hindi, or in the local language of the state where the product is sold. Safety warnings must not appear only in a language the buyer cannot read.

## IND-LAB-5: Importer Details
For imported products, the Indian importer's name and address must appear clearly on the label, along with the country of origin.
