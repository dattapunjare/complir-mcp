# EU Safety Rules (mock)
> FICTIONAL MOCK RULES for a student project. Not real law.

## EU-SAF-1: CE marking
Electrical products such as chargers, and toys, must carry the CE mark on the product or its packaging before they are sold in the EU. The mark shows the maker declares the product meets EU safety requirements.

## EU-SAF-2: Small parts warning for toys
Toys that contain small parts and are meant for children under 3 years old are not allowed. Toys with small parts for older children must carry a clear choking hazard warning on the packaging.

## EU-SAF-3: Test report for safety marks
A product that carries a safety mark such as CE must have a test report on file from an accredited laboratory. The report must be available to authorities on request.

## EU-SAF-4: Candle burn warnings
Candles must carry basic burn safety warnings, such as never leaving a burning candle unattended and keeping it away from children and pets.
