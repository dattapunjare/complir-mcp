# US Safety Rules (mock)
> FICTIONAL MOCK RULES for a student project. Not real law.

## US-SAF-1: Electrical safety listing
Electrical products such as chargers must carry a recognised safety listing mark from an approved testing laboratory before they are sold in the US. Products without the mark may not be sold.

## US-SAF-2: Small parts warning for toys
Toys that contain small parts must not be sold for children under 3 years old. Toys with small parts for children aged 3 to 6 must carry a clear choking hazard warning on the packaging.

## US-SAF-3: Test report for listed products
A product that needs a safety listing must have a test report on file from an approved laboratory. The report must be available to authorities on request.

## US-SAF-4: Candle burn warnings
Candles must carry basic burn safety warnings, such as never leaving a burning candle unattended and keeping it away from children and pets.
