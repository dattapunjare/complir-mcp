# US Labelling Rules (mock)
> FICTIONAL MOCK RULES for a student project. Not real law.

## US-LAB-1: Required label fields
Every packaged consumer product sold in the US must show the product name, net quantity, a lot number, and the name and address of the manufacturer, packer or distributor. Cosmetics and supplements must also list ingredients.

## US-LAB-2: Prohibited disease claims
Labels must not claim that a product cures, treats or prevents a disease unless it is an approved drug. Words such as "cure", "treats", "miracle" or "stops hair loss" are not allowed on cosmetics or supplements.

## US-LAB-3: Substantiated claims
Any claim such as "clinically proven" must be backed by a test report kept on file. Without a report, the claim must be removed from the label.

## US-LAB-4: Net quantity units
The net quantity must be shown in both US customary units (ounces, pounds, fluid ounces) and metric units (g, kg, ml, l), placed on the front of the package.

## US-LAB-5: Language requirements
Required label information must be printed in English. A second language may be added, but all required information must still appear in English.
