# India Safety Rules (mock)
> FICTIONAL MOCK RULES for a student project. Not real law.

## IND-SAF-1: BIS certification
Electrical products such as chargers, and toys, must carry the BIS standard mark and a registration number before they are sold in India. Products without the mark cannot be imported or sold.

## IND-SAF-2: Small parts warning for toys
Toys that contain small parts must not be sold for children under 3 years old. Toys with small parts for older children must carry a clear choking hazard warning on the packaging.

## IND-SAF-3: Test report for BIS products
A product that needs the BIS mark must have a test report on file from a recognised laboratory. The report must be shown to authorities on request.

## IND-SAF-4: Candle burn warnings
Candles must carry basic burn safety warnings, such as never leaving a burning candle unattended and keeping it away from children and pets.
