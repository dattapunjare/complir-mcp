# EU Labelling Rules (mock)
> FICTIONAL MOCK RULES for a student project. Not real law.

## EU-LAB-1: Required label fields
Every consumer product sold in the EU must show the product name, net quantity, a batch number, and the name and address of the EU importer. Cosmetics and supplements must also list ingredients.

## EU-LAB-2: Prohibited medical claims
Labels must not claim that a product cures, treats or prevents a disease. Words such as "cure", "treats", "miracle" or "stops hair loss" are not allowed.

## EU-LAB-3: Substantiated claims
Any claim such as "clinically proven" must be backed by a test report on file. Without a report, the claim must be removed.

## EU-LAB-4: Language Requirements
Product labels must provide the required information in a language that consumers in the country of sale can easily understand. Important safety, usage, and ingredient information must not be presented only in a foreign language.

## EU-LAB-5: Net Quantity Format
The net quantity must be clearly displayed using an appropriate metric unit, such as grams (g), kilograms (kg), millilitres (ml), or litres (L). The quantity must be easy to see and must not be misleading.

## EU-LAB-6: Batch Traceability
Each product must have a batch or lot number that allows the product to be traced back to its production batch. The batch number should be clearly printed and remain readable throughout the normal life of the product.

## EU-LAB-7: Importer Details
The EU importer’s name and address must be clearly shown on the product label or packaging. The information must be accurate and sufficient to identify the responsible importer.

## EU-LAB-8: Label Readability
Required label information must be clearly visible and readable. Text should not be hidden by packaging folds, stickers, or other design elements, and important information must not be presented in a way that could confuse consumers.

## EU-LAB-9: Ingredient Listing Order
For products that contain multiple ingredients, the ingredients must be listed under the heading Ingredients. They should be arranged from the ingredient used in the greatest amount to the ingredient used in the smallest amount.

## EU-LAB-10: Safety Warning Display
Any required safety warnings must be written in a language that consumers in the country of sale can understand. These warnings must also be clearly visible on the packaging without requiring the consumer to open the product.
